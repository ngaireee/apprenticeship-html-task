Team Roles in Venom

My team, Venom, is part of Commercial Data Services at Sainsbury's, and we are responsible for pricing and promotions data. Our goal is to create a singles source of truth for this data, ensuring it is accurate, reliable, and accessible to the business. This page outlines the various roles within Venom, each with differing, complimenting responsibilities.

Associate Engineer

As an Associate Engineer, I am one of the more junior members of the team, with fewer responsibilities compared to my more experienced colleagues. My role is primarily focused on learning, exploring new challenges, and contributing fresh perspectives. As a newcomer, I aim to question existing practices constructively and suggest innovative ideas. When I joined the team, my manager provided me with a month dedicated to self-directed learning, which allowed me to build foundational knowledge of our domain and services. Following that, I was assigned a project to take through the full Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), giving me hands-on experience and a deeper understanding of our workflows. This process also taught me how and when to seek guidance from senior team members. Now that I’ve settled into the team, I regularly pick up tickets like any other engineer. I typically focus on tasks with lower complexity or less time sensitivity, which allows me to continue building confidence and capability while contributing meaningfully to the team’s goals.

Engineer

Engineers make up the majority of my team and they are responsible for completing most of the development work. There are currently five engineers in my team, each with differing skill sets, that work together in a supportive way to cover our complex system of services. One of the Engineers I look up to within the team is Alex Lourinha. Alex has always been on hand to support my transition into the team, offering his expertise or pointing me towards another engineer that works on a certain corner of the domain more often. Alex, alongside the other engineers, helps to drive the completion of tickets, whilst taking on the responsibility of writing and maintaining robust services. It is also important that the engineers are commited to never walking past a problem, and invest effort in keeping their work upto date with technical advancements.

Senior Engineer

Senior Engineers play a key mentoring role within the team. They support junior engineers in their development and, alongside the Engineering Manager, help shape how the team operates. They also take ownership of system design tasks for projects within our domain. In our team, Abhishek Yadav is the current Senior Engineer, and we are in the process of hiring a second. Beyond mentoring and technical leadership, Abhishek takes on significant responsibility for managing incidents that impact our services. He communicates with other teams across Sainsbury’s that rely on our data and contributes to technical decisions that align with and complement the work of surrounding teams.

Engineering Manager

Engineering Managers are responsible for both the people and the projects within their teams. They play a crucial role in supporting team members’ growth, creating an inclusive culture, and ensuring our work aligns with broader business goals. In addition to managing individual performance and development, they coordinate with other tech teams to plan and deliver projects. Prajna Sinha is the Engineering Manager for my team. She leads the direction of our team by overseeing project timelines, and ensuring that we meet the exceptions set out by the business. Prajna also works closely with stakeholders across Sainsbury's to align our work with the wider business goals. Her technical background and leadership skills help maintain a balance between technical skill and team wellbeing, enabling us to grow as engineers while delivering quality solutions.

Product Manager

Product Managers (product owners) are responsible for the products produced or managed by the team. They have the most interaction with the business, and help to bridge the gap between business goals, user stories, and technical limitations. Jonas Amaah is the Senior Product Manager for our team. Jonas is present in all of our team's sprint ceremonies, and regular meetings to both ensure that our work meets commitments set by the business, and that technical difficulties can be communicated to stakeholders swiftly when they arise, to catch problems early and save waste time and money. Not having a technical background, Jonas brings the voice of end user to our discussions, whilst still having a strong understanding of the complexities of our services that he can communicate to stakeholders. As the product owner of these services, Jonas also takes the responsibility of keeping our priorities in order, and helps to protect our team from being pulled away from the product vision.

Staff Engineer

Staff Engineers typically work across multiple teams, offering technical mentorship and helping lead strategic change across tech. They hold responsibilities for things such as system architectures, engineering cultures and practices, for teams that sit across the business. We currently have a staff engineer, Brian Forkan, as member of our team as we take on the first step of the Core Commercial Transformation (CCT). This is large company-wide transformation project that will see us move away from legacy systems, towards a future-fit solution. Brian is currently in the process of defining and documenting how this transformation will impact our services. This will then allow him to develop a strategic plan to mitigate disruptions, and identify areas where we can introduce breaking changes now to optimize long-term efficiency and adaptability. His leadership ensures that our technical decisions are forward-looking and aligned with the broader transformation goals.
